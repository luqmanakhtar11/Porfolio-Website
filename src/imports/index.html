The challenge

Complex money flows, without the complexity.

TaifPay serves people with very different financial needs. The design had to make identity, wallet type, permissions, transfers, and recovery feel like one coherent product—not a collection of separate forms.

Design question

How might a financial app feel simple while still being explicit about risk, identity, and account responsibility?

The answer was not to hide complexity. It was to reveal only the right decision, at the right moment, with clear states before and after money moves.

01

Orient before asking

Explain why information is needed before requesting identity, permissions, or business documents.

02

Progress over paperwork

Split long setup journeys into short, visible steps with recoverable progress.

03

Make status unmistakable

Use consistent language and visual feedback for pending, successful, blocked, and failed actions.

Product architecture

A role-aware wallet ecosystem.

The core interaction model stays familiar while requirements adapt to the responsibility of each account—from fast personal access to document-heavy company setup.

Design approach

From journeys to a reusable system.

The completed file shows a process centered on coverage: map the journey, design the happy path, define exceptions, and package patterns for handoff.

01 / Map

Trace user intent

Organize screens by user goal rather than by isolated feature.

02 / Reduce

Stage information

Group questions into clear steps and postpone secondary decisions.

03 / Stress-test

Design the edges

Include errors, permission denial, loading, empty, and recovery states.

04 / Systemize

Prepare for scale

Reuse controls and patterns across every wallet context.

Key decisions

Confidence is a design feature.

Financial UX succeeds when users can predict what happens next. Three patterns do most of that work in TaifPay: contextual setup, progressive verification, and explicit transaction states.

Choose the wallet before completing the paperwork.

Up-front account context prevents users from entering a generic flow only to discover different requirements later.

Select wallet type

Continue

Break verification into visible, recoverable steps.

Step indicators and focused forms make a long compliance journey feel finite and easier to resume.

Verify your business

Documents are encrypted and used only for verification.
Save & continue

Treat every state as part of the transaction.

Success, errors, delays, and permission blocks explain what happened and offer a useful next action.

Visual system

A calm interface for high-stakes actions.
